# The-Nepal-Cricket-Index

An independent cricket dashboard by [Howzat Analytics](https://howzatanalytics.com/) for exploring Nepal Premier League (NPL) and Nepal national-team data.

## What you can explore

### Nepal Premier League

Explore NPL seasons, teams, players and match scorecards. The site brings together batting, bowling and fielding statistics, season standings and individual performance records.

Compare players using batting and bowling radar charts, with team and season filters for each player. Radar metrics are normalized against players in the selected team and season, so cards with different filters use different comparison groups.

### Nepal national team

Browse Nepal national-team player profiles, matches and scorecards, and explore the tournaments represented in the dataset.

## About the data

The index brings together match, innings, team and player records prepared from [Cricsheet](https://cricsheet.org/) data. The records have been transformed and aggregated to support the statistics and visualizations on the site.

Coverage reflects the available dataset and is not necessarily complete. NPL standings are derived from recorded league matches and are provisional when a season is incomplete.

The national-team dataset contains no matches against Afghanistan. Cricsheet has withheld Afghan men's matches; see its [explanation](https://cricsheet.org/article/explanation-for-withholding-of-afghanistani-matches/) for the decision.

For dot-ball percentages, batting figures use dot balls as a share of balls faced, while bowling figures use dot balls as a share of legal balls bowled.
