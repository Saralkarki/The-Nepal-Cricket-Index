About this project

Nepal Cricket Dataset

An independent, data-led resource for exploring Nepal cricket through match, team and player records.

The data

From prepared records to useful insight

The current release focuses on Nepal Premier League seasons represented in the dataset. Prepared Parquet exports are imported into a read-only analytical database, where match, innings, team and player records power the pages and statistics in this dashboard.

Player records include Cricsheet player identifiers for identity matching. Source-level attribution and licensing details are still being confirmed, so the dashboard does not claim that every record is a direct official feed or that the dataset is complete. Data coverage will be made clearer as provenance is verified and additional competitions are added.
