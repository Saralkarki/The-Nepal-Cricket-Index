About this project

The-Nepal-Cricket-Index

An independent, data-led resource for exploring Nepal cricket through match, team and player records.

The data

From prepared records to useful insight

The dashboard covers Nepal Premier League seasons and Nepal national-team matches represented in the dataset. Prepared Parquet exports are imported into a read-only analytical database, where match, innings, team and player records power the pages and statistics in this dashboard.

Match data is sourced from Cricsheet and has been prepared, transformed and aggregated for this dashboard. Player records include Cricsheet player identifiers for identity matching. See Cricsheet's licensing page for the applicable licences: most data uses the Open Data Commons Attribution License, while XML match data uses Creative Commons Attribution 4.0. This dashboard is independent of Cricsheet and does not claim that its coverage is complete.

The national-team dataset contains no matches against Afghanistan. Cricsheet has withheld Afghan men's matches in protest at the lack of support for Afghanistan's women cricketers. We support Cricsheet's stance; read its explanation for withholding the matches.
